The brainstorm

Nine ways seismic ideas could help

Each card pairs an open problem that World Labs or the wider field describes with a seismic-imaging idea, offered as a complementary idea (nothing here evaluates anyone's products), and says honestly what already exists, what this project tested, and what would come next. These are hypotheses to examine, not claims of new methods. New to the methods? Start with the illustrated primer.

What you will find

A filterable board of ideas. Each shows the open problem it addresses, the seismic idea behind it, existing work, our evidence so far, and the next step.

Key takeaway

The best-supported ideas are about trust and identifiability: saying what a generated world or a fitted physical parameter does and does not tell you. Most other ideas already have strong prior work.

Two termsSpatial intelligence here means understanding and generating 3D scenes from images, as World Labs' world models do. Physical AI means agents, such as robots, that act in the physical world and need those scenes to be trustworthy and physically meaningful.

How these were chosen

Each idea has a documented need (quoted from World Labs' public pages where one exists) and a seismic method that plausibly applies. The "already exists" lines come from the verified literature map; "not found in this search" never means "does not exist". Ideas that look already covered are included on purpose, so that nothing is presented as new when it is not.
