What already exists

Seismic imaging and spatial AI share much of their mathematics, and many bridges have already been built in both directions. This page pairs each seismic concept with its closest existing counterparts so that nothing in this project is presented as new when it is not. It describes work; it takes no position against anyone's.

What you will learn

What already exists for each seismic concept, and which references were verified against arXiv or Crossref.

Key takeaway

Most transfers are done or active. Open niches are narrow and mostly unproven.

How to read the evidence labelsVerified means title, first author, year and identifier were fetched from arXiv or Crossref and matched. Partial means a related version was verified but not the exact item. "Not found in this search" never means "does not exist": conference-only, industry and non-English work can be missed. Statements of opinion or hypothesis are marked as such in the source documents.
